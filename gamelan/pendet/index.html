Two lines share a stem. Jegog and Calung were recorded together, as were Kajar and the gongs, so those pairs play the same audio.

Pitch. Every stem is shifted up 100 cents from the original recording, measured against our gamelan rather than guessed: the two were about a semitone apart. Tempo is untouched. What remains is 12–21 cents per tone, the difference in how the two gamelans are tuned internally, which no single shift can remove.

Muted strikes are the italic figures on the two gangsa lines — eight of them, always polos and sangsih together, at m3 and m5 on beat 4 and twice in m25.

Bars 82–83 are 3/4. Timings come from the beat map fitted to the recording, so a line may begin a few tens of milliseconds early or late where the tempo moves.
