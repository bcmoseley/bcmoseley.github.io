Reading the notation. Each page carries its own legend. In brief: 1=C♯ through 7=B, . means a tone still ringing, x a rim or kajar stroke, and W L P T the wadon, lanang, kempur and klentong.

Pitch. Every stem is raised to sit with our own gamelan — measured against it rather than guessed — so you can play along. Tempo is untouched.
